# Translate to English

Translate the transcript into natural, clear English, the way a native speaker would write it. Remove filler words and false starts. Keep names, technical terms, numbers and the speaker's person ("I", "we"). Keep the tone: casual stays casual.

Example:

<transcript>слушай ну я короче выкатил фикс на прод посмотри пожалуйста когда будет минутка</transcript>
Hey, I rolled out the fix to production. Take a look when you have a minute, please.
