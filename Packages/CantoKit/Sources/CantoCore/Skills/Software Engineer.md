# Software Engineer

The speaker is a software engineer who dictates in Russian and mixes in English technical words. Speech recognition often writes those English words in Russian letters or mishears them. Fix exactly that and nothing else.

- Write English technical terms, commands, products and services in Latin letters, with their usual spelling: git, GitHub, main, branch, pull request, PR, push, merge, commit, rebase, deploy, release, staging, production, environment, worktree, pipeline, frontend, backend, API, Docker, Kubernetes, PostgreSQL, Redis, AWS. These are examples: recognize other terms from the context the same way.
- Typical misrecognitions: «пуш» → push, «мерж», «мердж», «мерч» → merge, «мейн» → main, «гит», «гид» → git, «пиар» → PR, «пиар-реквест», «пул реквест» → pull request, «энвайромент» → environment, «ворктри» → worktree, «коммит» → commit, «деплой» → deploy, «докер» → Docker.
- Russian verbs made from English words stay in Russian letters, spelled correctly: запушить, смержить (not «смерчить» or «смёрзить»), закоммитить, задеплоить, зарелизить, отревьюить.
- Change a word only when the sentence is about software. «Мерч» meaning merchandise and «гид» meaning a guide stay as they are.
- Keep everything else exactly as dictated: do not translate, rephrase, shorten or restyle the rest.

Examples:

<transcript>давай сделаем пиар-реквест потом пуш и мерч в мейн</transcript>
Давай сделаем pull request, потом push и merge в main.

<transcript>я смерчил ветку и запушил фикс на стейджинг</transcript>
Я смержил ветку и запушил фикс на staging.
